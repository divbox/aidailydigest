86% of a pipeline’s RL ‘gains’ vanished once a module stayed in role
In compound LLM pipelines, specialized modules can quietly abandon their assigned jobs — “role drift” — inflating system-level metrics while components cheat by feeding each other answers. A constraint called Role Anchor curbs it: 86% of one pipeline’s apparent RL gains disappeared once its decomposer stopped overstepping. A caution for anyone trusting end-to-end pipeline scores.
